\section*{Controller-Science Notation and Native Vocabulary}

\paragraph{Principle.}
OpenDSS/AltDSS class and property names remain canonical wherever practical.
Mathematical notation provides a controller-facing abstraction but must preserve an
explicit mapping to the corresponding OpenDSSSim registry identifiers.

\paragraph{Action layers.}
Let
\[
  a^{\mathrm{req}}_t
\]
denote the action requested by a controller,
\[
  a^{\mathrm{app}}_t
\]
the action admitted/applied by the explicit plant action-resolution policy, and
\[
  a^{\mathrm{real}}_t
\]
the realized post-solve physical/control value.

No equality between these quantities is assumed unless the frozen plant policy establishes
it.

\paragraph{State and observation.}
The full plant state and the controller observation are distinct. A controller consumes
only the state/observation view authorized by the frozen \texttt{ProblemSpec}.

\paragraph{Deferred symbols.}
P1 electrical variables, P2 graph variables, horizon indices, forecast notation,
constraint sets, objectives, rewards, and controller-specific decision variables are
intentionally deferred until the source-backed C1--C3 contracts.
